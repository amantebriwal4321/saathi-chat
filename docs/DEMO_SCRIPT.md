# Saathi demo script

About two minutes. Record a phone or a narrow browser for the elder, and a second window for the family. Room code SAATHI. Elder speaks Hindi. Family reads English. Chrome, with the microphone allowed.

## Scene 1. Landing (0:00 to 0:12)

Show the Saathi home screen. Hold on the headline and the phone preview.

Voiceover: This is Saathi, a family chat for elders. One big button. Their language. The people who worry about them.

## Scene 2. Two windows (0:12 to 0:28)

Open `/demo`. Click "Open as Ammi", then "Open as Aman" so the family window appears beside it.

Voiceover: Ammi joins as an elder, in Hindi. Her son Aman joins the same room, in English. Same room code, two seats, no passwords.

## Scene 3. Elder speaks Hindi (0:28 to 0:50)

On the elder window, press and hold the orange button. Say: "बेटा, आज मैंने दवाई ले ली।" Release. The line appears in large type.

Voiceover: She holds the button and talks. The browser hears Hindi and sends the words through Nexconn, tagged with her language.

## Scene 4. Family sees the translation (0:50 to 1:08)

Cut to the family window. Point at the Hindi line, then the English line under it.

Voiceover: Aman does not need to read Hindi. The original stays, and English sits underneath it. Nothing is thrown away.

## Scene 5. Family replies (1:08 to 1:24)

In the family composer, type: "That is good. I will call you tonight." Send.

Voiceover: He replies in English, in the same family room. It is a normal chat on his side.

## Scene 6. Elder hears it (1:24 to 1:40)

Back to Ammi. The reply is on screen in Hindi, and the phone reads it aloud. Tap Play once so the line repeats.

Voiceover: Her phone reads his reply in Hindi. If she missed it, one Play button says it again.

## Scene 7. Check-in (1:40 to 1:58)

Tap the green "I am okay today" button. Then tap the red "I need help" button. Show the family window: the red banner and the check-in card with the time.

Voiceover: Once a day she can say she is fine, or that she needs help. Help stays lit for the family until she checks in okay again.

## Scene 8. Call button (1:58 to 2:10)

If `ENABLE_CALLS` is on, tap Video call on the elder screen and Answer on the family screen. If the flag is off, show the elder screen and say the line below without pretending a call connected.

Voiceover: When video is turned on in the Nexconn console, this same screen can ring a family member. Until then, the words already get through.
